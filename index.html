About
I've been where you are.
"Most divorce coaches went through a divorce. I went through a high-conflict one — in California, in San Mateo County — and spent eight years learning everything the hard way so I could help others avoid that."

I'm not a therapist, a mediator, or an attorney. I'm someone who has navigated California's family court system, coordinated with mental health providers, argued over holiday schedules, built co-parenting communication frameworks from scratch, and learned what works and what makes things worse.

I have an MBA and a background in institutional finance — which means I can also help you think clearly about the financial dimensions of separation: support calculations, separation agreements, what to watch for.

I started helping friends informally. Then friends of friends. People who were lost in a process nobody explains, paying attorneys $400/hour to answer questions I could have answered for free. This service is the formal version of that.

What I bring
  • 8 years of firsthand experience in California family court — San Mateo County specifically
  • Hundreds of hours working with family law attorneys — I know what to ask and what to document
  • Real co-parenting experience: parallel parenting, holiday frameworks, summer schedules, school coordination
  • Mental health provider coordination — building a support team that actually works
  • MBA and institutional finance background — I can decode separation agreements and financial terms
  • Two daughters, now teenagers — I know what co-parenting looks like over years, not just the early crisis
